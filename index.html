👋About Me

I am an Associate Researcher in the Laboratory of Operations Research and Information at the Institute of Applied Mathematics, Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

Before joining AMSS in March 2024, I spent nearly six years at Microsoft Research Asia, most recently as a Principal Researcher. I received my Ph.D. in 2018 from the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, advised by Prof. Zhi-Ming Ma (Academician of CAS), and my B.S. in Mathematics from Shandong University in 2013.

My research sits between the mathematics of machine learning and its use in the physical sciences: the optimization dynamics and generalization theory of deep networks on one side, and AI for Science — neural operators, physics-informed models, and learned PDE solvers — on the other. I am a co-author of LightGBM, one of the most widely deployed gradient boosting frameworks.

I am recruiting postdocs interested in machine learning theory and AI for Science. Please get in touch if this sounds like you.

🔬Research Directions

Machine Learning Theory

Distributed and asynchronous optimization, optimization methods for deep learning, and the convergence and generalization properties behind them — including implicit regularization, adaptive methods such as Adam, and information-theoretic generalization bounds.

OptimizationGeneralizationDistributed Training

AI for Science

Accelerating scientific computing with machine learning: neural operators, physics-informed neural networks and equivariant architectures, applied to fluid dynamics, weather simulation, molecular modeling, and scientific discovery.
